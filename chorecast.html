How it works

  1. Someone posts a bet: a promise, a goal, a plan or just for fun. A judge is named up front.
  2. Everyone except the judge stakes 5–30 chips on YES or NO. The odds show what the family really expects.
  3. The judge settles it. Losers' chips go to the winners, and 10 chips buys one chore from someone who's behind.

House rules

  1. The judge is named when a bet is posted. The judge can't stake on it, and only the judge can settle or void it.
  2. You can only back yourself. On a promise or goal, the person it's about can bet YES but never NO.
  3. Stakes are 5–30 chips and locked once placed. You can't stake more than you have available.
  4. Payouts: losers' chips are split among the winners by stake. The judge keeps 5% for their trouble. If nobody took the other side, everyone gets their chips back.
  5. 1 chore = 10 chips, about 15 minutes of an agreed job. When someone does a chore they owe you, you tick it off, and 10 chips move back to them.
